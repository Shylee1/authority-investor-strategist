# Exhaustive Actionable Plan Template

Fill every relevant section for the chosen plan type. Leave no ambiguity.

## 1. Strategic Vision and Finish Line
- One-sentence desired end-state
- Success criteria (measurable)
- Explicit user constraints (verbatim)
- Opportunities to exceed expectations
- Trajectory awareness note

## 2. Current-State Analysis Summary
- Strengths with evidence
- Gaps with evidence
- Competitive or peer benchmarks (researched)
- Investor-perception risk areas

## 3. Overall Strategic Narrative
- Core positioning and story
- Key messages and proof points
- Audience map (investors, media, peers, public)

## 4. Immediate Actions (next 7-14 days)
- Numbered, concrete, owner-ready tasks
- Required assets or content
- Success signal for each

## 5. Sustained and Guided Actions
- Recurring activities with recommended cadence
- Effectiveness metrics and review triggers
- Adjustment rules based on data

## 6. Long-Term Foundational Actions (3-12+ months)
- Authority and thought-leadership compounding plays
- Presence and appearance infrastructure
- Investable-signal systems
- Stability and risk-reduction measures

## 7. Sub-Agent Task Breakdown
- Parallel research streams
- Content and asset production
- Monitoring and analysis
- Validation

## 8. Automation Designs
- Short-term automations
- Medium-term automations
- Long-term / recurring automations
- Status and alert delivery method

## 9. Success Metrics and Review Cadence
- Leading and lagging indicators
- Review schedule
- Kill or double-down criteria

## 10. Knowledge-Base Updates Required
- New investor methods or signals discovered
- New PR/social/authority tactics
- SEO / AEO / GEO / AI-agent visibility notes

## 11. Pre-Approval Checklist
- [ ] Plan type confirmed with user
- [ ] Every selected domain covered
- [ ] Immediate, sustained, and long-term actions present
- [ ] Sub-agent and automation opportunities identified
- [ ] Research logged
- [ ] Ready for user approval
